Enter the wonderful world of Chiikawa!

You've probably seen these tiny creatures in the internet before, and we're here to talk all about them! Nanka Chiisakute Kawaii Yatsu, also known as Chiikawa, is a Japanese manga series written by artist Nagano. It revolves around Chiikawa, Hachiware, and Usagi, who navigate a whimsical, yet unexpectedly dark world.

meet the characters!

Get to know the sweet cast of the world of Chiikawa

Chiikawa

The shy, gentle, and easily frightened main character. Though they cry often, Chiikawa has a pure heart of gold and always tries their best to protect their dearest friends.

Hachiware

The incredibly friendly, optimistic, and talkative tuxedo cat who lives in a cozy cave. Hachiware loves cooking delicious meals, playing the guitar, and comforting Chiikawa.

Usagi

The eccentric, energetic rabbit who expresses himself through loud squeals like 'Yaha!' and 'Ura!'. Usagi is surprisingly highly skilled at tasks and possesses immense courage (my GOAT).

Rakko

The highly-skilled sea otter who is a top monster hunter and a respecter mentor of Chiikawa and Hachiware.

Kurimanju

A senior-like chestnut-shaped creature who appreciates the finer, cozy things in life. Often seen enjoying savoury snacks, beer, and acting like a peaceful wise elder.

Shisa

The diligent, polite lion-dog inspired by Okinawa's traditional guardians, Shisa is extremely hard-working, loves eating ramen, and is studying intensely to obtain certifications.
